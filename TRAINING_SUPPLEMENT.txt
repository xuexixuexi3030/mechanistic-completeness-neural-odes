TRAINING AND REPRODUCTION NOTES

Experiment coverage
- Native: 40 configurations across five seeds (42, 123, 2024, 3407, 7777),
  giving 200 runs; observation noise levels are 0%, 1%, 6% and 10%.
- Ablations: 59 seed-42 configurations, including 55 without an initial
  checkpoint and four PG-NODE continuations described in EXTERNAL_WEIGHTS.txt.
- Matched controls: 72 configurations per seed (1031, 70595, 250333), giving
  216 runs across two systems, three model labels, two completeness levels,
  two guidance settings and three noise levels (0%, 1%, 6%). MC and MA labels
  in this suite are not independent replicates.

Settings that affect reproduction
Keep each suite's implementation and settings together. Native and ablation
configuration, data and evaluation behavior differ; matched controls retain
their seed-specific implementations. Use the supplied YAML files or matched
protocol.py files for initializations, loss weights and training budgets.

For seed 250333, twelve eco full-RHS forward variants involve resumed training
whose earlier-stage source provenance is incomplete. The supplied code retains
the available implementation; a fresh run is not a verified reconstruction of
those twelve archived results.

This release includes final experiment metrics and aggregate results under
results/. Old checkpoints and individual training logs are excluded. Training
creates new outputs. Sensitivity outputs are provided, while sensitivity
analysis and manuscript figure-assembly scripts are outside the code release.

Validation scope
All 115 Python files passed syntax checks, all 99 YAML configurations loaded
and built models, and all 216 generated matched configurations were checked
against archived settings. Thirty-four paired CPU single-step checks matched
the original code's weights and numerical outputs. This does not constitute
full-budget retraining of all 475 archived runs.
